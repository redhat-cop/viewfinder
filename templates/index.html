Template Usage Tips

Customization: All templates contain placeholders (highlighted in yellow when viewed) that should be replaced with customer-specific information. Search for [brackets] to find all placeholders.

Printing: Templates are optimized for both screen viewing and printing. Use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) to generate PDFs.

Branding: Templates include Red Hat branding. Modify as needed for co-branded deliverables with partners.
