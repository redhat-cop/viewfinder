Digital Sovereignty Maturity Assessment

Short Format: Focused 2-hour assessment session

Session Details

Duration: 2 hours

Format: Virtual or on-site

Date: [To be scheduled]

Focus: Assessment-only format with brief introduction

⚠️ Note: This shortened format focuses on completing the assessment quickly. For a comprehensive workshop including context-setting, detailed domain education, and in-depth results analysis, consider the Full-Day Workshop format instead.

Required Participants

  • Essential: CIO/CTO, CISO, Cloud Infrastructure Lead
  • Recommended: Chief Architect, Compliance Officer, Operations Manager
  • Red Hat: Digital Sovereignty Architect, Account Team

Detailed Agenda

0:00 - 0:15 (15 min)
Introduction & Framework Overview
  • Quick introductions and participant roles
  • Brief overview of the 5-level maturity model
  • Introduction to 7 Digital Sovereignty domains
  • Assessment methodology and scoring explanation
  • Selection of industry profile (LOB weighting)
  • Ground rules for efficient assessment
0:15 - 1:45 (90 min)
Domain Assessment (All 7 Domains)

Rapid assessment across all 7 domains (~15-18 minutes per domain):

  1. Data Sovereignty - Residency, protection, key management, data access
  2. Technical Sovereignty (including Open Source sub-pillar) - Stack ownership, vendor lock-in, portability, open source strategy
  3. Operational Sovereignty (including Managed Services sub-pillar) - Skills, processes, autonomy, DR/BC, vendor controls, exit planning
  4. Assurance Sovereignty - Audits, monitoring, compliance verification
  5. Executive Oversight - Governance, budget, accountability

Note: Open Source and Managed Services are assessed as integrated sub-pillars within Technical and Operational Sovereignty respectively.

For each domain:

  • Focus on Foundation tier questions (1-3) first - these are mandatory
  • Address Strategic tier questions (4-6) if time permits
  • Note Advanced tier questions (7-8) for follow-up if running short on time
  • Request evidence but keep discussions brief
  • Flag items for deeper discussion in follow-up
1:45 - 2:00 (15 min)
Quick Results Review & Next Steps
  • Display spider chart with overall results
  • Highlight top 3 strengths and top 3 gaps
  • Identify 2-3 quick wins
  • Schedule comprehensive results review session (recommended within 1 week)
  • Export results for detailed analysis
  • Next steps and follow-up actions

Time Management Tips

  • Strict time limits: ~2 minutes per question maximum
  • Evidence-based scoring: Ask for quick examples, not detailed explanations
  • "Parking lot" discussions: Note detailed topics for follow-up rather than exploring now
  • Focus on Foundation tier: Complete questions 1-3 in each domain first, then tackle Strategic/Advanced if time permits
  • Pre-work helps: If participants review domain descriptions beforehand, assessment goes faster

Pre-Session Preparation

To expedite the assessment, please have ready:

  • Current cloud provider(s) and regions in use
  • Data classification framework (if exists)
  • List of key vendors with data access
  • Compliance certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2, etc.)
  • Disaster recovery approach summary

Expected Outcomes

  • Initial baseline: Maturity scores across 7 domains
  • Visual results: Spider chart showing relative strengths/gaps
  • High-level insights: Preliminary understanding of sovereignty posture
  • Follow-up plan: Scheduled detailed review and roadmap session
💡 Recommendation: Schedule a 1-hour follow-up session within 1 week to review detailed results, discuss implications, and develop prioritized improvement roadmap.

Follow-up Options

After completing this short assessment, consider:

  • Results Deep-Dive (1 hour): Detailed review of scores, gap analysis, prioritization
  • Roadmap Planning (2 hours): Develop actionable improvement plan with timelines
  • Domain-Specific Workshops: Deep-dive into specific domains with low scores
  • Full-Day Workshop: Comprehensive sovereignty education and planning session
