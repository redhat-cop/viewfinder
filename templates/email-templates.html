Template 2: Pre-Assessment Preparation Email

To: [Workshop Participants]
Cc: [Red Hat Team, Customer Coordinators]
Subject: Preparation for Digital Sovereignty Assessment - [Date]

Dear Workshop Participants,

Thank you for confirming your participation in the upcoming Digital Sovereignty Maturity Assessment. I'm looking forward to our session on [Date].

Session Logistics:

  • Date: [Day, Date]
  • Time: [Start Time - End Time] [Timezone]
  • Location: [Address / Virtual Meeting Link]
  • Duration: [X hours] including breaks

What to Expect:

We'll conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your organization's capabilities across 7 Digital Sovereignty domains:

  1. Data Sovereignty - Control over data location, protection, and access
  2. Technical Sovereignty - Independence from vendor lock-in
  3. Operational Sovereignty - Autonomous operations without external dependencies
  4. Assurance Sovereignty - Ability to verify security and compliance
  5. Open Source - Strategic use of open source for independence
  6. Executive Oversight - Governance and accountability
  7. Managed Services - Sovereign control of external services

Pre-Session Preparation:

To maximize the value of our time together, please gather the following information (if available):

  • Current cloud infrastructure provider(s) and services being used
  • List of critical business applications and their hosting locations
  • Existing compliance frameworks and certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2, FedRAMP, etc.)
  • Data classification policies and data residency requirements
  • Key vendor relationships and managed service provider agreements
  • Recent security audits, risk assessments, or compliance reviews
  • Disaster recovery and business continuity documentation
  • Open source usage policies and practices (if applicable)
  • Any known sovereignty concerns or regulatory requirements

Note: Don't worry if you don't have all of these items. We'll work with what's available and identify gaps during the assessment.

Assessment Approach:

This is a collaborative, evidence-based assessment. We'll:

  • Review each domain's capabilities
  • Discuss your current state with specific examples
  • Rate maturity on a 5-level scale (Initial → Managed → Defined → Quantitatively Managed → Optimizing)
  • Identify both strengths and improvement opportunities

Attached Materials:

  • Workshop Agenda (detailed schedule)
  • Maturity Model Overview (framework explanation)
  • [Add any other relevant materials]

If you have any questions before the session, please don't hesitate to reach out. See you on [Date]!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Contact Information]
